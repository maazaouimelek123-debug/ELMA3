Our story

Most clothes are made for everyone. The same shirt, the same colors, the same slogan — made by the thousands, for no one in particular. ELMA3 turns that around. We start with you, and make exactly one.

Why we exist.

Clothing says something before you do. Yet almost everything on the rack was designed to sell to as many people as possible, which means it says very little about any one of them. We think what you wear should be able to carry your words, your jokes, your language, your people — the things that make you, you.

What we build.

So we build two things. Premium blanks worth wearing for years — heavyweight tees, fleece hoodies and crewnecks, canvas totes and caps. And ELMA3 Studio, a design tool right in your browser, where you choose the color and add text, graphics and your own images, front and back. Then we make that one piece, just for you.

One blank tee. Five people. No two alike.

    FAQ

    Questions? Answers.

    Can’t find what you’re looking for? Contact us

    How long does shipping take, and what does it cost?

    Every piece is printed to order, so it leaves us a few business days after you place your order. Standard delivery anywhere in Tunisia is 8 DT, and it’s free on orders over 150 DT. You’ll see the exact cost in your bag before you check out.

    Can I return or exchange my order?

    Yes. Returns are free within 30 days of delivery — if your piece isn’t quite right, or you’d like a different size, just send it back. And if something arrives damaged, misprinted or not as you designed it, get in touch and we’ll make it right with a reprint or a refund.

    How do I find my size?

    Tees, hoodies and crewnecks have a relaxed, slightly boxy fit. The size guide in ELMA3 Studio lists the measurements for every size. Between two sizes? Size down for a closer fit, or stay true to size for the relaxed look. Totes and caps are one size, and the cap has an adjustable strap.

    How should I care for my piece?

    Wash cold and inside out with similar colors. Tumble dry low or hang to dry. Skip the bleach and don’t iron directly on the print — your design will stay vivid for much longer.

    How are designs printed, and how long do they last?

    We print with water-based inks directly onto the fabric, then heat-cure every print so the ink bonds with the fibers. The result feels soft, breathes like the rest of the garment and holds up through many washes when cared for as above.

    Can I upload my own images?

    Yes — add your own photos and artwork to the front or back in ELMA3 Studio. High-resolution PNGs with a transparent background look best. Please upload only images you own or have permission to use. We refuse orders with content that is hateful, harassing, violent or otherwise offensive, or that infringes someone else’s rights.

    Can I change my order after I place it?

    Before you check out, you can reopen any piece in your bag and keep editing it in the Studio. Once you’ve ordered, printing starts quickly — contact us as soon as possible, and if your piece hasn’t been printed yet, we’ll do our best to update it.

    Which payment methods do you accept?

    This storefront is a demo: checkout doesn’t take real payments, and no card details are collected or stored. When ELMA3 opens for real orders, the accepted payment methods will be listed at checkout.
